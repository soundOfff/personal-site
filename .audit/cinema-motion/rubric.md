# Rubric, 1 to 5

1. Film-specific invention. A recognizable spatial/behavioral idea for each reel, with grounded alternatives beyond generic visual decoration.
2. Visible improvement and cohesion. Both reels feel richer and distinctive while extending the existing film world coherently.
3. Reader agency and usability. Content, nav, keyboard, touch, mobile and reduced-motion remain clear; nothing blocks, hijacks or unnecessarily delays interaction.
4. Motion/runtime quality. Fluid transitions, stable geometry, bounded work and reliable mount/dispose/reel switching.
5. Maintainability. Small owned data shape and integration, clear lifecycle and restrained implementation within the six-file scope.
