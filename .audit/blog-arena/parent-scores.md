# Parent comparison before independent verdict

Read all candidate source and rationale end to end. Rendered all three archive and article designs, in both color modes across the views. All three builds passed.

A is the preferred base. Its compact navigation, consistent serif hierarchy, 660px article measure and explicit scoped neutral tokens create a focused publication. Its readable metadata and native controls are a substantial improvement over the baseline. B is close, with good semantic article markup and filter restoration, but its sans archive and taller mobile navigation introduce a second typographic/layout idea without a strong benefit for this brief. C improves details but keeps most of the baseline's tiny metadata and interface-like body type; its print colors are incomplete in saved dark mode.

Potential grafts from B are its unnumbered compact archive introduction, popstate filter restoration, semantically simpler article wrapper and explicit table label. Avoid combining both visual systems. C's local overflow focus styling is already present in A. No independent C graft is necessary if final testing confirms that convergence.
