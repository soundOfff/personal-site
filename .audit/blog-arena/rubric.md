# Rubric, score 1 to 5

1. Long-form readability. Clear prose rhythm, comfortable measure/type, useful headings and readable metadata in both themes.
2. Minimal editorial identity. One coherent distinctive publication across index and articles; restraint, no film effects, clutter or generic card dashboard.
3. Browsing/navigation. Understandable archive, working tag links/filter, obvious article/back navigation and useful contents without noise.
4. Robustness/accessibility. Mobile geometry, wide technical content, keyboard/focus, light/dark/print and semantic structure.
5. Maintainability/integration. Small clear ownership in shared layout/styles, no film/bootstrap leakage, unchanged content/SEO/routes and no unnecessary dependencies or abstractions.
