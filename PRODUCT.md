# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering hiring managers, recruiters, founders and CTOs assessing Tomas Brasca's ability to ship AI products end to end. They browse the work, try the portfolio agent, and look for a direct contact route.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio with five film interpretations of the same work, plus a quiet default view. The work and the person must remain easy to find through every interpretation.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, tactile, restrained. A visitor opens a small independent cinema program on their laptop, with patient lighting and a sense of place rather than a dashboard.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS cards, busy controls, rapid chasing lights, repeated self-description, and explanatory scene annotations crowding the work.

## Design Principles

- Keep the film atmosphere while simplifying the interface.
- Let the work speak; show the name without repeated role descriptions.
- Use current, verified project facts, including private projects without pretending their source is public.
- Make motion patient, responsive, and optional.
- Preserve the established art direction of each film.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic navigation, keyboard controls, visible focus and readable contrast. Respect reduced motion by skipping the cinema countdown and decorative movement.
